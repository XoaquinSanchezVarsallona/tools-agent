source: Apple Human Interface Guidelines
section: Foundations - Design principles
url: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/
---

Apple interface design favors clarity, deference, and depth. Interfaces should make content primary, keep controls understandable, and use visual hierarchy to show what matters now. Components should feel direct and responsive, with labels that describe the result of an action.

For generated components, prioritize obvious affordances, concise text, visible state changes, and consistent interaction behavior across default, pressed, disabled, and loading states.
