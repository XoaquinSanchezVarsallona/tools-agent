Material buttons communicate actions users can take. Primary buttons represent the most important action, while secondary buttons support lower emphasis alternatives. Button styling should clearly distinguish enabled, disabled, hover, focus, pressed, and loading states.

Buttons should use legible typography, adequate padding, visible focus outlines, and sufficient contrast. Loading buttons should prevent repeated activation and keep the label area stable while progress is shown.
