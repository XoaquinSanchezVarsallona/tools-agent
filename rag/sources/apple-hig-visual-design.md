source: Apple Human Interface Guidelines
section: Foundations - Color, typography, and layout
url: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/
---

Apple visual design uses color intentionally to communicate state, selection, and emphasis. Typography should support scanning with clear weight and size differences, while layout should use alignment, spacing, and grouping to reduce cognitive load.

Controls should maintain comfortable touch targets, readable contrast, and restrained styling. Disabled states should reduce emphasis without disappearing. Loading states should preserve layout stability and avoid ambiguous actions.
